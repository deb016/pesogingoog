About PESO Gingoog City

Mandate

The Public Employment Service Office (PESO) is a non-fee-charging, multi-employment service facility established under Republic Act No. 8759, the PESO Act of 1999. It is linked with the provincial and regional offices of the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) for coordination and technical supervision, and with the DOLE central office as part of the national employment service network. PESO Gingoog City is committed to the prompt and efficient delivery of employment facilitation services, along with timely labor market information and DOLE program updates.

Vision

A Gingoog City that provides reliable, sustainable, and inclusive employment facilitation services — contributing to poverty alleviation and the city's overall economic development.

Mission

To promote employment opportunities through referral for local and overseas job placement, livelihood and self-employment assistance, employability enhancement training, and career and employment coaching — improving the quality of life of Gingoognons.

This portal digitizes registration, job browsing, application, and referral processing for PESO Gingoog City, while walk-in employers now register and get verified online as well, in line with our current system design.
